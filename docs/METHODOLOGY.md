# Benchmark methodology
Five indices: IDX30 (Indonesia), SET50 (Thailand), VN30 (Vietnam), PSEI (Philippines), FBM KLCI (Malaysia). Preserve three separately trained arms: historical effective-date PIT membership, a June 2026 roster applied backward, and date-specific PIT/current intersection control. Exact common support instead matches predictions from the first two fitted arms; it is not the separately trained intersection arm.

Target: next valid same-security observation's raw open-to-close direction, with a maximum 14-calendar-day gap. This implementation is broader than an unconditional next-exchange-session target. Nine lagged raw-OHLC/same-market features; no adjusted close or volume. Expanding same-market history, initially 60 training dates, 20 calibration-gap dates, 20 test dates, 20-date step. Training labels must mature before calibration starts. Logistic imputation/scaling is fit inside the training pipeline. RF/LightGBM use fixed registered parameters, with seeds 11, 23, 37, 53, 71. No new hyperparameter search was introduced.

The primary aggregate gives equal weight to securities within date, dates within interval, intervals within market and the five markets. Full-support current-minus-PIT Brier inference uses 10,000 paired interval-confined circular moving-block replicates, block length 20, seed 271828. BH adjusts the four-model family; supported differences require both the interval and adjusted-p gate. Operational coverage is separate. No supported difference is not equivalence. Four-market block-40 inference remains exploratory because IDX30 is unavailable.

Original scripts and parameter YAMLs retain their historical paths. The small `papers/` trees supply explicitly imported feature/model helpers; no results from those papers are included. The feature helper's SHA-256 equals the hash frozen in the V8 configuration. Model wrappers are recovered current refactor dependencies, not represented as hash-authenticated members of the original tagged archive.
